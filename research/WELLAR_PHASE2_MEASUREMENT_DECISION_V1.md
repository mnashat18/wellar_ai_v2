# Wellar V2 — Phase 2.1 Measurement Decision v1

**Status: PROPOSED — not approved for participant enrollment or training.**  
**Decision scope:** define a candidate pre-work occupational-fatigue target and its measurement/governance gates. This is a research decision record, not evidence of predictor validity.

## Decision

**Primary candidate:** the **pre-shift perceived intershift-recovery state**: the worker's self-reported recovery from work-related fatigue accumulated during the previous work period, measured before the next shift. Use the authorized Arabic **OFER-15 Intershift Recovery (IR) subscale** as a continuous reference score; preserve the instrument's native direction and score. Do not reverse it or create a fatigue class unless an instrument expert and statistician approve and preregister the mapping. This is a candidate operationalization of pre-work occupational fatigue burden, not a momentary fatigue scale. The IR score is a recovery construct, and its equivalence to residual fatigue at the next shift must be approved before it can serve as the primary target.

OFER-15 is the strongest occupationally specific candidate in this shortlist, and its three subscales distinguish acute, chronic and intershift-recovery constructs. Arabic psychometric evidence is from Lebanese nurses, not office/customer-service staff: the 2,037-person Rasch analysis supported reliability for the **Chronic Fatigue** subscale for low-to-moderate screening, but found disordered response categories, item dependence and inter-subscale correlations contrary to expectations; it concluded construct validity needs further investigation. It does not establish the IR subscale as a valid pre-shift criterion in Wellar's intended workforce. The Arabic translation study reports obtaining permission from the original author; that permission is not presumed transferable to Wellar or commercial use. [OFER-15 development](https://digital.library.adelaide.edu.au/items/259e3b53-463b-4024-bf22-eff1124858f0), [A-OFER Rasch study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31068491/), [Arabic translation study](https://scholarworks.aub.edu.lb/bitstreams/3614b248-da55-4832-b4b2-f582da5ee371/download).

**Fallback candidate:** Arabic **Fatigue Assessment Scale (FAS-Ar)**, scored continuously under its authorized scoring instructions, only as a general fatigue measure / feasibility comparator if A-OFER IR is rejected or unavailable. It is retrospective (past two weeks), not pre-shift or occupationally specific, and Arabic validation was in a hearing-rehabilitation sample; a translated item was removed in that validation. Therefore it is **not an interchangeable fallback label** for the primary target and cannot satisfy the occupational-fatigue target without a qualified reviewer explicitly revising the estimand. The rights holder permits clinical research with attribution, while company/commercial use of the FAS or adaptations requires a one-off payment per questionnaire/translation; confirm terms for this exact study before use. [Arabic FAS validation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35767227/), [FAS rights and conditions](https://www.ildcare.nl/en/fatigue-assessment-scale-conditions-for-use-and-copyright/).

### Shortlist comparison

| Instrument | Time / construct | Pre-shift fit and Arabic evidence | Rights / disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A-OFER-15** | Work-related acute fatigue, chronic fatigue, intershift recovery; shift/work-period and recovery framing, not a pure momentary state measure. | Best occupational fit. Arabic evidence is Lebanese nurses; CF findings do not validate IR or generalize to office/customer-service employees. | Obtain exact Arabic wording, scoring, research and commercial-use permissions. **Primary candidate: IR**, conditional on expert approval of the construct and timing. |
| **FAS-Ar** | General fatigue, retrospective (two-week frame); not a distinct mental-versus-physical occupational score. | Arabic translation/psychometrics reported in Jordanian/Saudi hearing-rehabilitation participants; population and time window do not establish pre-shift occupational validity. | Rights holder publishes research and commercial conditions, including a commercial-use fee. **Fallback only**, not equivalent target. |
| **ROF-Ar** | Momentary perceived fatigue intensity, including exercise and recovery contexts; not a work-specific fatigue instrument. | Recent Arabic validation is exercise/recovery based in Tunisian adults; not office/customer-service work or pre-shift occupational validation. | The validation article is open access; that alone does not establish permission for the scale wording or commercial adaptation. Not selected. [Arabic ROF study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13282412/). |
| **SOFI / SOFI-15** | Multidimensional subjective work-fatigue experience, commonly tied to work exposure. | No fit-for-purpose Arabic validation for this population/time point established by the reviewed evidence. | Rights and authorized Arabic version unresolved. Not selected. |

No instrument in this shortlist currently establishes a validated, momentary measure of occupational fatigue immediately before work for Wellar's office/customer-service population. Qualified review may reject both candidates; do not silently substitute KSS, PVT, an ad hoc single item, or V2-derived features.

## Separate reference outcomes and interpretation

- **Primary target candidate:** A-OFER IR continuous score as above. Store the reference as a distinct target from sleepiness and vigilance. Whether a higher IR score maps to lower residual fatigue is a construct hypothesis for review, not a label conversion approved here.
- **KSS:** retain the ordinal 1–9 score and timestamp as a separate subjective-sleepiness reference. Compare fatigue-score/KSS disagreement and inspect its direction, timing, missingness and repeatability as construct-discordance evidence. Disagreement is not deception or dishonesty and must not be used for participant discipline or exclusion.
- **Validated PVT:** record trial-level data in restricted reference storage and prespecified latency/lapse summaries in the manifest as a separate vigilance-performance outcome. Select a validated app, protocol, device and latency treatment before enrollment. Compare PVT/KSS/fatigue patterns to assess distinct constructs and label inconsistency; a PVT discrepancy does not invalidate self-report or establish a false answer. Do not use Wellar's five-click check as a PVT reference.

## Minimum pilot-session reference sequence

Freeze a single protocol and client/API contract first. For each session: (1) create random participant/session/sample IDs; log coded site/device/OS, consent-scope reference, protocol version and UTC clock offset/uncertainty; (2) record session clock and synchronized event timestamps; (3) capture permitted media in a fixed order with actual start/end, technical metadata and modality quality/missing reason; (4) within a prespecified short interval (pilot protocol proposes at most two minutes), administer KSS and the authorized A-OFER version in a fixed or preregistered randomized order, retaining item/scale completion timestamps; (5) administer the selected validated PVT immediately afterward with fixed instructions, duration, rest, hardware/app and invalid-trial rules, retaining trial data in restricted storage; (6) record interruptions, deviations, missingness, quality failures and withdrawal without silently dropping the session. Preserve actual timestamps and mark each target temporally unmatched when its preregistered time bound is exceeded. The protocol's 5-second media captures are proposed local-protocol details; verify/freeze the actual client/API capture contract before enrollment.

## Required dataset fields

Use `WELLAR_DATASET_MANIFEST_SPEC_V1.md` semantics: one participant-session-target record (or normalized equivalent), UTF-8 JSONL only as an index, never a media store. Keep identity/contact, participant linkage, signed consent, PVT trials, and any item-level fatigue responses in separately controlled vaults.

- **Participant-level, minimized:** random non-derived participant pseudonym; approved eligibility/cohort code and only prespecified broad demographic/language bins needed for validity assessment; recruitment/consent-vault references and withdrawal status. No names, employee/account IDs, contact details, exact worksite or precise schedules. Participant-to-split mapping stays with the independent custodian.
- **Session-level:** random session/sample IDs and sequence; pre/post-shift context; coded site, device, OS, industry/occupation; protocol version/hash; UTC collection and per-event timestamps; clock offset and uncertainty; consent version/status and modality/purpose booleans; protocol deviations, interruption and withdrawal/missingness reason.
- **Per modality:** availability and explicit missing reason; capture interval, duration, format, dimensions, FPS/sample rate as applicable; quality status; feature values/nulls and coverage; capture/client/API/extractor/feature-map/worker versions, non-secret config digest and processing time; opaque raw-media reference only when consent/retention permit. No media, biometric templates, filenames or direct media checksums in the manifest.
- **Per reference target:** KSS instrument/version, ordinal value, timestamp and status; occupational-fatigue instrument/subscale/version, scoring algorithm/version, raw responses kept restricted, approved raw/scored continuous value, scale range/units, timestamp and status; PVT app/device/protocol/version, start/end, prespecified metrics, invalid/missing status and restricted trial-data reference/hash. Every absent value has an explicit state and reason; never impute “normal.”

## Measurable gates before Phase 3 collection/qualification

Phase 3 participant collection remains **blocked** until every gate below is documented as passed:

1. **Construct/instrument gate:** an independent occupational-fatigue researcher and Arabic psychometrician approve in writing the pre-shift estimand, A-OFER IR (or replacement), time window, Arabic wording/dialect, scoring direction, target-population applicability, and cognitive-interview plan. If evidence is inadequate, complete an approved instrument adaptation/validation protocol before treating it as a criterion.
2. **Rights/approval gate:** written permission covers exact instrument translation, administration, scoring, reproduction in the study app, research/evaluation, storage and any proposed commercial use; licensing and data-use agreements are on file. Obtain local IRB/ethics and privacy/legal approvals, employer/site access approval, non-coercive consent and separate modality/purpose scopes. Establish named PI, safety escalation, independent data/identity/split custodians and withdrawal contact.
3. **Measurement/protocol gate:** approve the validated PVT and KSS sequence; freeze reference order, timing bounds, missingness/invalid-trial rules, population and recruitment criteria, device/site strata and protocol/client/API versions. An independent statistician signs a participant-level repeated-measures sample-size/precision plan and feasibility assumptions before outcomes are examined.
4. **Governance/technical dry-run gate:** validate the manifest schema against synthetic records; **100%** of required schema/provenance/consent fields must validate, and **zero** direct identifiers or media must appear in analysis manifests. Demonstrate consent-scope rejection, participant-disjoint split assignment, coded metadata minimization, encrypted restricted storage/access logs, retention expiry and deletion/withdrawal across primary and backup stores using synthetic objects only. Hash and freeze protocol and schema.
5. **Qualification gate before any model training:** an independent custodian reports aggregate participant/session counts and target-specific missingness, timing matches, modality-quality coverage, score range/variation, deviations and split counts. A statistician and scientific panel must attest that prespecified participant-level precision/power, outcome support, leakage controls, and held-out site/device requirements are met. No arbitrary sample count or performance percentage is set here; derive numerical margins and sample size from approved expert inputs before enrollment. Dataset eligibility is target-specific; passing collection readiness alone is not training authorization.

The exact unresolved approvals are: **(a)** construct and A-OFER IR time-window/population fit; **(b)** authorized Arabic version and reproduction/research/commercial rights; **(c)** independent reviewer sign-off and cognitive testing; **(d)** local ethics/privacy/legal and site/employer approvals, lawful basis and consent; **(e)** retention durations, deletion/withdrawal and custodians; **(f)** validated PVT app/device/protocol and KSS order/timing; **(g)** recruitment/eligibility, safety escalation, sample-size plan and meaningful margins; **(h)** frozen capture/API and manifest mapping; **(i)** secure storage, access, split and external-validation plan. Until these are closed, no real participant enrollment, collection, training, production inference or participant/employer feedback is authorized by this record.

The pilot protocol's candidate and collection sequence are useful drafts; its unresolved target timing is resolved here only as a **proposal**, pending review. The manifest's consent, provenance, explicit-missingness, retention and split-vault rules are retained. Scientific acceptance remains target-specific: no model training or efficacy conclusion follows from Phase 0/1 technical tests or this record. KSS, occupational fatigue and PVT remain distinct outcomes; association between them is neither equivalence nor a truthfulness test.

**Disposition:** **PROPOSED** measurement decision. Change to **APPROVED** only after qualified scientific review and instrument/ethics/privacy/site permissions are complete. Until then, participant enrollment and Phase 3 are **BLOCKED**.
