# Wellar V2 Pre-Work Fatigue Pilot Protocol v1

**Status:** draft for ethics, measurement, and operational review. This is a protocol, not evidence that any Wellar predictor is valid. No participant data may be collected under this draft until all listed decisions, permissions, and approvals are complete.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Assess whether the de-identified, descriptive features produced from Wellar V2's existing short video, still image, and short audio inputs contain reproducible information about three distinct reference outcomes in the intended pre-work population. The study is observational; no prediction is returned to participants, employers, supervisors, or operational systems. Do not use results to make employment, fitness-for-duty, clinical, or safety decisions.

The V2 intake limits currently documented in code are video MP4 up to 30 seconds / 20 MiB, image JPEG or PNG up to 4 million pixels / 5 MiB, audio WAV up to 30 seconds / 8 MiB, with video sampled at 4 Hz. The local self-test capture implementation specifically makes a 5-second MP4 (ideal camera dimensions 640x480), one JPEG camera frame (minimum 320x240), and a 5-second microphone WAV encoded as mono 16-bit PCM at the browser's native sample rate. Those are self-test implementation details, not proof of every production client's behavior; freeze and verify the actual client/API contract before enrollment. Relevant code: `app/core/config.py`, `app/media/storage.py`, `app/media/extractors.py`, `app/self_test.html`.

## 2. Separate estimands and outcomes

Each outcome is a separate research question and, if later justified, a separate model. Never treat one as a proxy label for another.

1. **Subjective sleepiness:** KSS item score 1–9, recorded as an ordinal value and timestamped. Preserve the original score; define any ordinal grouping in a separate preregistered rule. KSS measures perceived sleepiness, not occupational fatigue.
2. **Occupational fatigue:** one independently validated, licensed/permission-cleared fatigue instrument and its prespecified score/subscale. Preferred candidate for assessment is the Arabic OFER-15 (A-OFER), but it is not approved as the study criterion until its use is licensed and its content, time window, population fit, and Arabic measurement properties are reviewed. A-OFER covers acute fatigue, chronic fatigue, and intershift recovery; the published Lebanese nurse sample found psychometric limitations, including lack of unidimensionality, item dependence, and limited targeting. Acute fatigue's retrospective work-period framing is not automatically a valid *pre-work momentary* outcome. Decide whether the target is pre-shift intershift recovery/occupational fatigue, or whether a suitably validated state instrument is needed. Do not relabel a single ad hoc question as validated occupational fatigue.
3. **Vigilance performance:** PVT reaction-time observations and prespecified continuous summaries (at minimum median/mean response latency and lapse count using the chosen instrument's published definition). Any impaired/not-impaired class requires an independently justified and preregistered rule. The V2 self-test's five-click task explicitly is not a validated PVT and must not serve as this reference. Prefer a validated touchscreen PVT implementation; record app/version, hardware, OS, latency correction, device, protocol duration, and invalid-trial rules.

Primary estimands, primary fatigue subscale, KSS timing, PVT duration and lapse rule are **unresolved preregistration fields**. Do not select them after looking at feature-outcome associations.

## 3. Candidate fatigue instruments and Arabic requirements

| Candidate | Construct and fit | Arabic evidence / limitation | Protocol disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arabic OFER-15 (A-OFER) | Work-related acute fatigue, chronic fatigue, and intershift recovery; most directly occupational among these candidates. | Arabic translation and permission are reported in the Lebanese nurse research. The Rasch analysis found multidimensionality/item dependence and cautioned about score interpretation; evidence in nurses does not automatically generalize to Wellar's workforce or pre-shift momentary fatigue. | **First candidate for review, not yet selected.** Obtain authorized wording/scoring and licensing terms; have occupational-health and Arabic psychometrics reviewers decide which subscale/time window matches the intended pre-work construct. Assess cognitive comprehension and measurement properties in the intended population. |
| SOFI / SOFI-15 | Multidimensional work-related fatigue experience, often assessed after work. | Evidence exists for Swedish and Spanish adaptations; this review did not establish an Arabic validation suitable for this intended population and moment. | Alternative only if Arabic translation rights and local cultural/psychometric validation are prospectively done. |
| Fatigue Assessment Scale (FAS) | General fatigue severity rather than specifically acute pre-work occupational fatigue. | An Arabic FSS exists, but FSS is not the same instrument as FAS; do not conflate names. No adequate fit-for-purpose Arabic FAS evidence established for this protocol. | Not the default occupational criterion; investigate only if a qualified instrument expert supports the construct/time window. |
| Arabic Rating-of-Fatigue (ROF-Ar) | Perceived fatigue around graded exercise/recovery. | Recent Arabic adaptation/validation is exercise-context evidence, not automatically occupational or pre-work validation. | Not interchangeable with occupational fatigue; possible supplementary candidate only after review. |

Before choosing: (a) obtain author/publisher permission and exact authorized Arabic version and scoring; (b) identify whether the measure is state, shift-recall, or trait; (c) conduct cognitive interviews in the target dialect/literacy groups; (d) preregister structural validity, reliability, floor/ceiling, responsiveness and convergent hypotheses as appropriate; (e) test measurement invariance/DIF across key intended subgroups when sample supports it. Translation alone is not validation. A-OFER candidate sources: [Arabic OFER Rasch analysis](https://scholarworks.aub.edu.lb/server/api/core/bitstreams/73f34fb7-25cf-45c7-90b4-b12bac869fd9/content), [Arabic questionnaire translation and nurse study](https://scholarworks.aub.edu.lb/bitstreams/3614b248-da55-4832-b4b2-f582da5ee371/download). Instrument evidence review: [2024 measurement-properties review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nhs.13070). PVT reference: [NASA Fatigue Countermeasures Laboratory](https://www.nasa.gov/human-systems-integration-division/human-performance/fatigue-countermeasures-laboratory/).

## 4. Design, participants, and schedule

Prospective repeated-measures observational pilot in the intended Wellar user/workforce population, at more than one site if feasible. Enrollment is not authorized until local ethics/privacy review, employer/site permissions, instrument licensing, data governance, and the sample-size analysis are approved. Define eligibility/exclusion, safety escalation for a participant reporting severe sleepiness, and recruitment protections without coercion. Participation and withdrawal must have no employment consequence.

Collect multiple sessions per participant on different pre-work days/shifts to capture within-person variation. A post-shift session may be added as a separately identified temporal context to test instrument responsiveness, but it must not be pooled or relabeled as pre-work. Plan at least one site/device wholly reserved for external validation if recruitment allows. Record shift type and time since prior shift using coded categories.

## 5. Standardized session procedure and synchronization

Use a single study app/client build and frozen API/media contract. Do not add longer recordings or alter the V2 media contract for this pilot. Ask participants to sit comfortably, face the front camera, remove no safety equipment required by their normal work, and avoid coaching facial expression. Provide a neutral standardized instruction. Do not infer health state from the recording.

1. Create a random session ID and record pseudonymous participant ID, site/device/OS codes, protocol version, and UTC clock-sync status. Do not record names in the research manifest.
2. Start a monotonic session clock and capture UTC start/end timestamps plus device monotonic offsets for every event. Synchronize the recording device clock against the study time source before each session; record measured offset and uncertainty. Use server receipt timestamps only as an additional transport timestamp, not the measurement time.
3. Capture **video** first or in a fixed, preregistered order: one 5-second front-camera MP4 at the client's supported settings (the local self-test uses ideal 640x480, camera video without audio). The operational extractor samples at configured 4 Hz. Record actual codec, dimensions, FPS, duration, bytes, and extractor sampling metadata. No PERCLOS label is computed by current V2.
4. Capture **image** as one front-camera JPEG still using the client's supported method (self-test waits 500 ms after camera readiness; its minimum accepted size is 320x240). Record dimensions, encoding, capture timestamp, and whether it is a distinct capture or a selected video frame. Never silently treat a selected frame as an independent observation. If the production client contract differs, document and freeze that contract before enrollment.
5. Capture **audio** as one 5-second WAV sample, mono 16-bit PCM at the device's native sample rate, following one approved standard Arabic reading prompt. Keep the prompt identical across sessions; record prompt ID, language, sample rate and exact start/end. The current V2 extractor computes waveform-quality features only, not vocal fatigue. Do not include names, workplace details, free speech, or third-party voices in the prompt/room.
6. Within a fixed short interval after media capture (target ≤2 minutes; record actual elapsed time), administer KSS and the selected authorized fatigue instrument, in a fixed order or a randomized order specified before data collection. Record each item response time and instrument version. Do not coach or show model output.
7. Administer the selected validated touchscreen PVT immediately after questionnaires, with a prespecified rest/instruction period and fixed duration/device/app. Record trial-level stimulus and response times, invalid trials, interruptions, and device latency metadata. Keep the target reference time explicit: PVT start/end and derived summary window. Do not substitute the V2 five-click browser check.
8. Log missing modality/label and reason, protocol deviations, interruptions, quality failures, and participant-requested withdrawal. Never silently discard a poor-quality or incomplete session. Whether it enters a given target's analysis is decided by the preregistered target-specific rule.

The measurement window is the media capture start through reference instrument/PVT completion. Since reference measures are not literally simultaneous, retain event timestamps and prespecify maximum allowable separation per target. If a limit is exceeded, mark that target reference as temporally unmatched; do not “correct” it by interpolation.

## 6. Media, consent, privacy, retention, and access

Obtain separate, plain-language and non-coercive consent for: (i) camera/video processing; (ii) still-image processing; (iii) microphone/audio processing; (iv) retention of raw media; (v) research feature extraction/evaluation; (vi) model training; and (vii) transfer to another site or commercial use. Declining one modality must not invalidate participation in other permitted modalities. Explain that face and voice may be biometric/sensitive, the purpose and limits of automated analysis, foreseeable risks, access/deletion procedures, withdrawal cutoff after irreversible aggregation, and that no operational prediction or employment decision will result. Obtain ethics and privacy approval and document lawful basis and jurisdictional requirements before collection.

Store identity/contact and the participant-ID linkage key separately from media and feature data, under a distinct custodian and access policy. Encrypt media in transit and at rest; use a restricted research storage area, least-privilege named access, MFA, audit trails, and approved de-identification. Do not put raw video, image, audio, names, employee numbers, exact worksite, or linkage key in JSONL manifests or model-development copies. Derived features can remain sensitive/biometric and require the same governance.

Retention must be a finite, ethics-approved period with a named owner, automatic expiry, and auditable deletion of media, backups, derived features, and linkage where applicable. Specify in consent the retention period and deletion process before enrollment; no default indefinite retention. On withdrawal, delete identifiable media and linkable records within the stated policy unless legally/ethically required to retain a minimal record; document irreversibly aggregated outputs that cannot be removed. Cross-site transfer requires explicit consent scope and written data-transfer agreement; default is prohibited.

## 7. Sample-size planning

Do not use an arbitrary number of clips or the software trainer's minimum participant count as a scientific sample-size justification. Before enrollment, a statistician independent of model development must calculate target-specific participant counts from: (a) a prespecified primary estimand and clinically/operationally meaningful error or association margin agreed by domain experts; (b) plausible within-person and between-person variance/reliability from external evidence or a blinded feasibility phase; (c) repeated sessions per participant and intraclass correlation; (d) class/score distribution and anticipated missingness/abstention; (e) site/device clusters; (f) desired confidence-interval precision and planned subgroup analyses; and (g) multiplicity across three targets. Report assumptions and sensitivity ranges. If no credible variance/margin exists, run a blinded feasibility/pilot solely to estimate recruitment, repeatability, missingness and variance; do not use it to assert predictive efficacy. Recalculate before locking a definitive test size and do not inspect locked-test outcome associations.

## 8. Split and model-development firewall

The custodian assigns **participants**, not rows, sessions, frames, or clips, to train, validation, or locked test before feature-outcome analysis. All sessions and all modalities from one participant stay together. Store the assignment key separately. Use group-aware, site/device-aware allocation constrained by the sample-size plan; never move a participant to fix class balance after viewing model results. Development data may use participant-grouped resampling within train/validation only. The final test is held by an independent custodian and released once after freezing protocol hash, feature extractor/version, model hash, preprocessing, calibration, decision rules, and abstention thresholds. Any test-informed change invalidates that test as locked.

In addition, reserve participants from an external device model or site entirely outside training/validation. Evaluate this external-device/site cohort independently (participants disjoint from all development data); report device holdout and site holdout separately. If the study lacks the number of sites/devices to support this, state that external generalization is untested. No cross-split media, perceptual duplicates, participant fingerprints, or repeated-session leakage is allowed.

## 9. Deviations and study stopping

Pause enrollment for consent/privacy breach, wrong media destination, clock/synchronization failure affecting reference timing, repeated protocol violations, or unexpected participant risk. The PI and data custodian document deviations without deleting inconvenient observations. This pilot does not authorize deployment or participant-level feedback. Continue only after corrective action and ethics approval where required.

## 10. Unresolved gates before enrollment

- Confirm the production Wellar client's exact capture settings/API media flow against the server contract; the local self-test is a reference implementation, not proof of production capture behavior.
- Select and license the exact Arabic occupational fatigue instrument/subscale and resolve its pre-work time-window validity.
- Select validated PVT app/device/duration, latency correction, lapse rule, and accessibility protocol.
- Approve population, recruitment, site/device strata, sample-size analysis, missingness/abstention rules, subgroup support minimums, and meaningful acceptance margins.
- Obtain ethics/privacy approvals, retention schedule, consent language, storage/access/deletion plan, and custodian assignments.
- Freeze protocol and manifest schemas/hashes before any data collection; no training or inference under this protocol draft.
