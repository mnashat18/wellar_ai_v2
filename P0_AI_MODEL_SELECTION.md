## Rights determination

The selected model repository declares MIT, but the model card links two datasets and neither linked dataset's rights/consent provenance was verified for this intended commercial deployment. A permissive model-repository license does not prove the right to use underlying training data for this use. Therefore “commercial license verified” is **NO** and the weights remain present only for local research/non-production evaluation. Do not deploy or market outputs.
